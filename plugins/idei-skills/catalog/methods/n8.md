# Grounded theory

Theory is derived from data by the constant comparative method (Glaser, Strauss, 1960s).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Open coding: read the data line by line and mark ideas.
2. Axial coding: refine the codes and compare new data with earlier data.
3. Group the codes into categories and find the core category.
4. Theoretical sampling: use the gaps to decide what data to look for next.
5. Keep notes (memo).
6. Continue until saturation, when no new ideas appear.
