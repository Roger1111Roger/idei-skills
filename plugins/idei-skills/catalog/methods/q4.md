# Nominal group technique

Ideas are written individually, then discussed and ranked by voting (Delbecq, Van de Ven, 1971).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. The facilitator explains the goal and the procedure.
2. Everyone silently writes down ideas for about 10 minutes.
3. Read the ideas aloud in turn without discussion.
4. Clarify the ideas and merge them into categories, discarding nothing.
5. Rank by voting.
