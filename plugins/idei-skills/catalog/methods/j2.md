# WSR: three views

Three views of a problem: objective facts (Wuli), ways of seeing, modeling and acting (Shili), relations between people (Renli) (Gu, Zhu, China). Each has its own regularities, and none reduces to another. The source does not describe steps, so here this is a set of questions.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Wuli: what objective facts and constraints are known?
2. Shili: how do we model the situation and what actions do we choose?
3. Renli: whose interests, relationships and habits affect the result?
4. Check that none of the three views is missed.
