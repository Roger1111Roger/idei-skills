# Delphi method

Anonymous rounds of surveying experts with feedback (Helmer, Dalkey, Rescher, RAND).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Assemble a group of knowledgeable experts.
2. Send out the questionnaire.
3. Consolidate the answers and comments, find points of agreement and disagreement.
4. Send out an anonymized summary; the experts revise their answers.
5. Repeat until a predefined stopping point: a number of rounds or a stable result.
