# Six Thinking Hats

The whole group wears one “hat” at the same time, that is, looks at the question in one role, then switches roles (de Bono).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Hats: white (facts), red (feelings), black (risks), yellow (benefits), green (ideas), blue (process control).
2. Everyone in the group wears the same hat at any given moment.
3. The facilitator with the blue hat sets the order and asks for summaries.
4. Do not overuse the black hat.
5. Record the decisions and actions.
