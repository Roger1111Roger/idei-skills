# Axiomatic design

Design as a chain of mappings: customer needs, functional requirements, design parameters (Suh, MIT). Independence axiom: a design parameter must not interfere with other functions. The first three steps are taken; manufacturing variables belong to implementation.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Translate customer needs into functional requirements.
2. Build a hierarchy of functions.
3. Choose a design parameter for each function.
4. Build a “functions to parameters” matrix and check that the links do not cross.
