# Biomimetics

Searching for solutions in living nature through the six steps of the “biomimicry spiral” (Biomimicry Institute).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Define: what impact and what criteria are needed.
2. Biologize: describe the functions and context in the language of biology.
3. Find natural models with the same function.
4. Abstract: reformulate the mechanism as a design strategy.
5. Apply: assemble concepts from the principles found.
6. Evaluate against the criteria and return to earlier steps.
