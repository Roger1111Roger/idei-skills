# Horizon scanning

Systematic search for early signs of change: weak signals, that is, emerging topics that may grow.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Watch the PESTLE areas: politics, economy, society, technology, law, environment.
2. Use different sources: research, media, interviews, other people's reviews, atypical sources.
3. Assess the signals: trends and possible consequences.
4. Repeat regularly.
5. Record the outcome in seminars, consultations, databases.
