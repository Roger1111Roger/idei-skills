# Crowdsourcing and citizen science

Scientific work with public participation: from small tasks (observation, classification) to jointly posing questions.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Decide the participants' role: data contribution or co-authorship.
2. Break the task into simple parts.
3. Give clear rules and an interface.
4. Collect the observations.
5. Verify with experts.
