# Scenario planning

Several plausible scenarios of the future for testing strategies.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Choose a focal question and a time horizon.
2. Identify the key forces and uncertainties.
3. Build 2-4 plausible scenarios, for example around the two main uncertainties.
4. Test the idea against them and find robust solutions.
