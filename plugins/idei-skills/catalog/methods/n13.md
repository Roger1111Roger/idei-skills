# AI hypothesis generation

AI proposes hypotheses based on the literature; a human assesses novelty and testability.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Formulate the research goal.
2. Ask the AI to generate hypotheses with justification from the literature.
3. Screen them for novelty and testability together with an expert.
4. Check the references manually.
