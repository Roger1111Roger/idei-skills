# Problematization instead of gap-spotting

Instead of looking for what is “not studied”, the hidden assumptions of the literature are challenged (Alvesson, Sandberg, 2011).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Write out the assumptions the literature rests on.
2. Choose a debatable assumption.
3. Formulate a question that challenges it.
4. Check that the question does not reduce to “nobody has studied this”.
