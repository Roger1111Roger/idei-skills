# How Might We

Formulating a problem as a question “how might we…”. The phrase became established in Basadur's work at Procter & Gamble as the one most accepted by teams for posing problems. The source does not say who invented it. The steps below are generally accepted: the page does not describe the procedure.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Take an observation about the user's problem.
2. Rewrite it as “How might we…?” without building in a solution.
3. Make several questions of different breadth.
4. Choose the question to generate ideas for.
