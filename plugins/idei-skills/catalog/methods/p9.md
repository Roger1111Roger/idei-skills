# Janusian thinking

The ability to hold two opposite ideas in mind as simultaneously true (Rothenberg). The steps below are taken from M. Mikhalko's account.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Turn the problem into a paradox: two opposite sides, both true.
2. Collapse the paradox into a two-word title.
3. Find an analogy in nature or another field.
4. Pick out its special property and transfer the equivalent to the problem.
5. Develop this into a new idea.
