# Forced connections

Deliberately linking two items that are not usually connected (Cocco, 1987; Osborn's example).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Define the item that needs to be changed.
2. Choose another, seemingly unrelated item.
3. Compare them and derive a meaning that at first seems distant.
