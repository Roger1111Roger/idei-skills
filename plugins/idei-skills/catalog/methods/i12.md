# Disney method

The team takes turns playing the dreamer, the realist and the critic (Dilts, 1994). The realist asks about feasibility, the critic looks for risks.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Dreamers come up with ideas without limits.
2. Realists evaluate and plan what is needed and whether it can be tested.
3. Critics constructively look for risks and gaps.
4. Open questions go back to the dreamers, and the cycle repeats.
