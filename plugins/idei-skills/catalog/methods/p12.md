# Random stimuli

A random word leads to the edge of the problem, from where you can see paths not available from the center (de Bono).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. State the problem clearly.
2. Take a random noun and use the first one that comes up.
3. Work with the word's concepts and properties; do not look for a simple match.
4. Do not change the word and do not build a chain of associations; keep the links that gave the idea.
