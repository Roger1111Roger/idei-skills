# AI and idea diversity

AI raises individual creativity but reduces the diversity of ideas in a group (Doshi, Hauser, 2024).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Generate some of the ideas without AI.
2. Ask the AI for different styles and roles.
3. Check the resulting diversity.
