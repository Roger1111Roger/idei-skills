# Incubation and insight

A pause helps problem solving, especially for ill-defined problems (meta-analysis by Sio, Ormerod, 2009).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Work on the problem, then deliberately step away.
2. During the pause, choose an undemanding activity.
3. Come back and write down what came to mind.
