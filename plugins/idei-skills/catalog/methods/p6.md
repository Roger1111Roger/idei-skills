# Bisociation

An idea arises at the intersection of two normally unconnected systems of thought (Koestler, 1964).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Choose two unconnected frames, for example taxi and game.
2. Write down what exists in both.
3. Build a new concept from the overlaps.
