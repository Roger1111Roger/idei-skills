# James Lind Alliance

Patients and doctors jointly rank unanswered questions.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Bring together patients and clinicians.
2. Collect unanswered questions.
3. Check against the literature that the question is truly open.
4. Rank jointly and pick out the main ones.
