# FINER criteria

A filter for a question: feasibility, interest, novelty, ethics, relevance (Hulley et al.).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Assess the question against each criterion.
2. Mark the criterion that fails.
3. Reformulate the question or postpone it.
