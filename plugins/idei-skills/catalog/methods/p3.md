# Types of creativity (Boden)

Creativity can be combinational, exploratory or transformational.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Write out the implicit rules of your field.
2. Combinational: combine familiar ideas in a new way.
3. Exploratory: reach the limits of the solution space.
4. Transformational: remove or replace one of the rules.
