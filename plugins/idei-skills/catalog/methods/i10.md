# Attribute listing

An object is broken down into attributes, variants are sought for each and then combined (described since the 1930s, Crawford, 1954). Too many variants result, so the 7–8 main attributes are taken.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Choose a product or process.
2. List its attributes: material, shape, market.
3. Keep the 7–8 most important.
4. Find alternatives for each.
5. Combine them and see whether a new approach appears.
