# Idea evaluation cards

Two cards from the Money + Magic approach: “big versus quick and doable” and “market versus destruction of the company”; each evaluates one criterion. Weak source: a student project. It also notes that the final choice still relies on intuition.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Choose a short list of ideas.
2. For each, ask: “what must be true for this to work”.
3. Place on the “big or quick and doable” map.
4. Place on the “market or destruction of the company” map.
5. Send the visible assumptions for checking.
