# KJ method

Idea cards are sorted into groups by similarity and labeled without discussion until the diagram is ready (Jiro Kawakita).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. State the topic clearly.
2. Everyone writes one idea per card.
3. Read the cards aloud and hang them on the wall.
4. Silently sort them into groups.
5. Label the groups and remove duplicates.
6. Use arrows to show links between groups.
