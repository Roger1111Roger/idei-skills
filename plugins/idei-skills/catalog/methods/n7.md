# Literature-based discovery

Hypotheses from links between two unconnected bodies of literature (Swanson, 1986).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Choose two bodies of literature.
2. Find shared intermediate terms.
3. Write out pairs that are absent from the direct literature.
4. Assess plausibility and check.
