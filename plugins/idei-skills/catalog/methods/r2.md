# Assumption map

Assumptions are formulated as testable hypotheses and placed by importance and by availability of evidence; important ones without evidence are tested first.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Write hypotheses as “We believe that…”: desirability, feasibility, viability.
2. Draw a 2×2 grid: importance and availability of evidence.
3. Place the hypotheses on the grid.
4. Pick the “important, no evidence” corner and devise checks for it.
