# Stage-Gate

Work proceeds in stages with gates between them (Cooper, 1993). A committee evaluates against a catalog of criteria, mandatory and desirable, and chooses: go, kill, hold or rework.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Describe the gate criteria in advance: mandatory and desirable.
2. At the end of a stage, collect the results and assess how much the uncertainty has decreased.
3. The committee decides: continue, stop, pause or rework.
4. Each next stage is more expensive than the previous one, so the gates come before costs grow.
