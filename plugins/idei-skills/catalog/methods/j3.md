# SECI (Nonaka, Takeuchi)

Knowledge is converted between tacit and explicit forms in four modes (Nonaka, Takeuchi, Japan). A project needs externalization: writing tacit knowledge down as concepts and a document. Caveats: the model grew out of Japanese firms, is rigidly ordered and does not explain how ideas themselves are born.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Socialization: sharing experience through observation and joint work.
2. Externalization: express knowledge in concepts, images or text so that others can use it.
3. Combination: assemble explicit knowledge into new forms.
4. Internalization: absorb explicit knowledge through practice.
5. For the spec, the main mode is externalization.
