# Hamming questions

Choosing an important problem that has an approach to it (Hamming, “You and Your Research”). The importance of a result does not make a problem important; a good approach is also needed.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Keep a list of 10-20 important problems in the field.
2. Ask: why are you not working on them?
3. What is needed for a good approach to one of them to appear?
