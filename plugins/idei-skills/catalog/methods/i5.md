# Cultural probes

Task kits (map, postcard, camera, diary) that people use to record their own lives in order to inspire designers (Gaver, Dunne, Pacenti, 1999). Poorly suited to a requirements list: the data is subjective and participants may not return the kit.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Assemble a kit with clear tasks.
2. Hand it out to participants and give them time.
3. Get the materials back.
4. Interpret as inspiration, not as statistics.
