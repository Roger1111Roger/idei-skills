# Lead users

Lead users face general market needs months or years before the rest and will benefit from a solution (von Hippel).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Define the characteristics of a lead user in your field.
2. Find a group of people with these characteristics.
3. Bring them together with the team and solve the problem jointly.
4. Check whether ordinary users value what was found.
