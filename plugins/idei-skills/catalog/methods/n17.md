# PICO

Structure of a clinical question: patient or problem, intervention, comparison, outcome. Helps to search for evidence more precisely.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. P: describe the patient or problem, not too narrowly.
2. I: name the intervention or exposure.
3. C: what it is compared with.
4. O: a measurable and important outcome.
