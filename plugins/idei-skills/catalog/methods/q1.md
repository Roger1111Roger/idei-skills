# Synectics

Deliberate metaphors and analogies show a familiar problem in an unfamiliar context (Gordon).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Make the familiar strange: each person writes about five metaphors for the phrase “Our problem is like…”.
2. Use analogies: direct (like a bird), personal (imagine being the object), compressed conflict (a pair of contradictory words).
3. Choose one or two of the most apt metaphors and explain the choice.
4. Together find hidden problems and new ideas.
