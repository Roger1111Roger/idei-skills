# Production blocking

In oral group brainstorming, participants wait for their turn and lose their thoughts, so such groups produce fewer ideas than the same people working separately (Diehl, Stroebe; Mullen et al., 1991).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Start with individual writing of ideas.
2. Combine and discuss afterwards.
