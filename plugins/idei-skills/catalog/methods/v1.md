# TELOS: five kinds of feasibility

Justification along five dimensions: technical, economic, legal, operational, schedule. Caveat: some of the source's statements have no references, and the technical and operational estimates are preliminary until there is a draft of the requirements.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Technical: can it be built, are the knowledge and resources available.
2. Economic: cost versus expected value, estimates for optimistic, pessimistic and neutral scenarios.
3. Legal: no conflict with legal requirements.
4. Operational: how well it solves the problem and fits into processes.
5. Schedule: whether the solution will be in time to be useful.
6. If no significant market is found, the source considers that there is no project.
