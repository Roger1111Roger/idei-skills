# SIT

Systematic Inventive Thinking: changing a product using five templates in a “closed world” (Goldenberg, Mazursky, Solomon, 1999).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. List the components of the product and its environment.
2. Apply a template: subtraction, multiplication, division, task unification or attribute dependency.
3. Assess where the modified version gives value.
