# Brainwriting 6-3-5

Six people each write three ideas in five minutes and pass the sheets around the circle: 108 ideas in 30 minutes (Rohrbach, 1968).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Each person writes three ideas in five minutes.
2. Pass the sheet to the neighbor.
3. The neighbor develops the ideas or adds new ones.
4. Repeat for six rounds, then group the ideas.
