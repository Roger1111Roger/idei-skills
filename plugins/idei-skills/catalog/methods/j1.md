# NM method (Nakayama)

Method of Masakazu Nakayama: an idea through analogy with nature or technology. It goes top-down from the required function, whereas in TRIZ the path starts from the situation. Secondary source (an article in TRIZ Journal); the assessment of the method is given by the article's author.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Key word: briefly name the required function with a verb.
2. Analogy question: find a phenomenon in nature or technology that provides this function.
3. Basis question: find out the principle or mechanism of this phenomenon.
4. Design question: transfer the principle to your own task and obtain ideas.
5. Assembly: combine the ideas into a concrete design, taking conditions and constraints into account.
