# Blue ocean strategy

Searching for a new market by asking four questions about the factors accepted in the industry (Kim, Mauborgne).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Draw the industry's strategy canvas.
2. Apply the four actions: eliminate, reduce, raise, create.
3. Find a factor that competitors do not have.
4. Check that there is demand for the new offering.
