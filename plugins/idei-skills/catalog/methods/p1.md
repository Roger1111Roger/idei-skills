# Wallas stages

A four-stage model of the creative process, described by Wallas in 1926.

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Preparation: gather material and define the problem.
2. Incubation: step away from the problem and do something else.
3. Illumination: write the idea down immediately when it comes.
4. Verification: evaluate and refine the idea.
