# C-K theory

Design as an expansion of the space of concepts (C) and the space of knowledge (K) (Hatchuel, Weil).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Write the initial concept in C with no known answer.
2. Separate what is already known (K) from what is not.
3. Expand C with new attributes, noting the knowledge needed.
4. Acquire knowledge until the concept becomes feasible.
