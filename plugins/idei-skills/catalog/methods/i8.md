# Lotus blossom

A central theme in a square and eight petals with ideas; each petal becomes the center of a new flower (Matsumura).

## How to apply (project suggestion)

1. Draw a square with eight circles around it.
2. Write the theme in the square.
3. Write eight ideas in the circles.
4. Make each idea the center of a new flower.
5. Repeat until the ideas run out.
