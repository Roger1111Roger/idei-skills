# Source rule

Every skill reads this before naming a method, approach or step. It prevents the main mistake: presenting a retelling or a project suggestion as the words of the method's author. Phrase everything below in the user's language.

## What the marks in the catalog mean

1. **Source link.** Each method, approach and step lists the pages that were read when the card was written. "Checked against these pages" means the card does not contradict them. It does not mean the author's own works were read.
2. **No link.** Say "not checked against a primary source".
3. **All methods are retellings by default.** The authors' original books and papers were mostly not read. When citing a method, add: "from a retelling, verify before quoting".

## How to phrase it

- Method name, then source, then the caveat if needed. Example: "Method: Wallas stages. Description checked against <link>; this is a retelling, not the author's own work."
- The "How to apply" section of a card, examples and the project's own rules are a **project suggestion**, not the words of the method's author.
- `extras.md` ("Do not forget") consists of project rules. Its source pages confirm individual principles, not a ready-made method.
- Do not put in quotation marks any phrase that is not in the card. Do not attribute words, years or numbers to an author unless they are in the card.
- Keep caveats written inside a card ("the source notes...", "not confirmed", "critics say...") when retelling. Do not soften or drop them.

## Knowledge outside the catalog

It may be used, but say so plainly: "this is not in the catalog and not verified". Do not add it to the catalog without reading its source.

## Known weak spots

- Approach a5 (first principles): the source gives only a definition.
- Approach a6 (gap analysis): the source is a blog, not research.
- Approach a10 (wild ideas): the source confirms the PO method; the 70/20/10 split is not confirmed by a source.
- Extra e10 (AI in research): no source.
- Extra e13 (limits of the check): no source, based on a single case.

The method cards, approaches, steps and extras are generated from the map (`map/idei.html`) by `tools/extract_catalog.js`. Edit methods in the map, not in the generated files. This file is written by hand and is not overwritten by the script.
