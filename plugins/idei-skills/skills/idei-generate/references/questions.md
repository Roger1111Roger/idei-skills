# Context questions (ask all ten in one message)

Number them. Tell the user: "Answer what you can. If you do not know, write 'don't know' or skip it; I will fill the gap with a labeled assumption and you can correct it later."

1. Field: what do you do, or in which area should the idea be?
2. For whom: who exactly is the idea for (a person, a role, a group)?
3. Pain: what hurts, or what do you want to improve? One concrete situation.
4. Existing: what exists today for this, and what was already tried?
5. Constraints: time, money, team size.
6. Assets: skills, tools, audience, contacts, data you can use.
7. Success: what result would you call a success, and by when?
8. Off limits: topics, rules, technologies or approaches you do not want.
9. Taste: what do you like or dislike in existing solutions (examples)?
10. Risk appetite: safe and proven, bold, or as wild as possible?

## Unknown answers

- If the user writes "don't know" or leaves a question out, fill it in yourself with a plausible, deliberately specific assumption. Free imagination is allowed here.
- Mark every such line `[assumed]`.
- Show the full context block once (user answers and assumed lines) and go on without waiting. The user may correct any line; if they do, rerun only the affected part.
- Assumptions about question 7 and 8 must be conservative (no hidden bans, no invented targets).
